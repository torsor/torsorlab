mathematical work with AI agents raises questions about understanding, evidence,
and continuity. this site is a home for research, writing, and tools around
those questions.

## what guides the work

**human direction.** questions and priorities can change as the mathematics
develops. a useful result can be a proof, an example, a better
question, or a failed route that shows where to look next.

**legible evidence.** claims retain their support, scope, and uncertainty.
writing and review help test an interpretation.

**continuity.** enough context remains for another person or agent to see what
happened, what remains open, and why a direction changed.

the tools serve these aims. writing and research about AI's role in mathematics,
learning, and collaboration are developing alongside them.

this work is maintained by <a href="https://dkrashen.org">Danny Krashen</a>
with AI agents.
