these are [tools](/tools/) for doing mathematics with AI agents, made with
the aim of keeping the work understandable to the people doing it.

maintained by <a href="https://torsor.org">Danny Krashen</a> with AI agents.

## citing torsorlab

if torsorlab helped with a paper, an acknowledgment that names the tool, the
model, and what it was used for is the most useful kind. for example:

> We used torsorlab (https://lab.torsor.org) with [model and version] to
> [check the exposition / compare the paper with the literature / review
> drafts]. The authors checked all suggestions and are responsible for the
> content.

to cite it as well:

```bibtex
@misc{torsorlab,
  author       = {Krashen, Daniel},
  title        = {torsorlab: tools for mathematics with {AI} agents},
  howpublished = {\url{https://lab.torsor.org}},
  year         = {2026},
  doi          = {10.5281/zenodo.24173125}
}
```

many journals also ask for a statement on the use of AI; the acknowledgment
above is written to serve as one.
