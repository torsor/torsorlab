`AGENTS.md` and `method/` carry a set of standing positions. they are what make
the room predictable to work in.

## who decides what

a **direct instruction to start or proceed authorizes that work.** it is not
answered with a proposal panel instead. proposal panels are for significant new
directions, when requested or when `method/proposals.md` calls for one — not
for a small example, a source check, a toy computation, or a bounded
exploration.

the agent **asks when a choice would materially select the mathematical
direction** or alter the intended claim, and continues independent useful work
in the meantime. it shares provisional thinking early enough for you to
redirect it, rather than presenting a finished line you did not choose.

and it **matches the scale and mode of the request** — exploration,
computation, drafting, cleaning, review, or decision are different activities,
and the reply should be in the one you asked for.

## how evidence is graded

five kinds are kept distinct: proposed arguments, finite computations, imported
theorems, cleaned exposition, and independently reviewed arguments. the
assumptions and the range that matter are stated. **a dependent conclusion
cannot be more secure than the input it relies on**, and is not recorded as
though it were.

mistakes are corrected promptly, and the dependencies they affect are
inspected.

the formal status ledger is used when you ask for a progress report, when
recording a session closeout, or when mathematical status has materially
changed. it is not appended to ordinary discussion.

## cleaning, and certification

**manuscript cleaning is collaborative.** cleaners can ask located questions
and propose edits; the author confirms that the cleaned text preserves the
intent. it is not a silent rewrite.

**certification is independent.** blind referees see an exact, self-contained
review unit, without author confidence signals or prior votes. review proceeds
by natural claims and applications, so an accepted conditional calculation is
not mistaken for verification of its hypotheses. reviewer dialogue uses focused
excerpts and keyed responses — a complete packet is resent only when the review
object materially changes.
