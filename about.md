## What the name covers

torsorlab is the working name for agentic AI collaboration in Danny Krashen's
research, teaching, and tooling. It is not an institution and has no members
beyond its collaborators; it is a way of naming work that was genuinely done
jointly, rather than pretending it was done alone or leaving it unattributed.

## How to credit it

A suggested acknowledgement:

> Portions of this work were developed in collaboration with torsorlab
> (lab.torsor.org), using agentic AI tooling.

Where a tool from this site did substantive work on a document, name the tool.

## What it does not cover

The mathematics is the author's. The lab names the tooling and the process, not
the results.
