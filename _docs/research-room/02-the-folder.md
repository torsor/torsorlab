| where | what belongs there |
|---|---|
| `AGENTS.md` | the working agreement and the reading order |
| `conventions.md` | definitions, notation, standing assumptions |
| `goals/` | the current questions, concise and editable |
| `journal.md` | chronological reasoning, and changes of direction |
| `notes/` | arguments, examples, computations, informative dead ends |
| `canonical/` | the claims currently load-bearing, and their support |
| `review/` | exact review units, reports, responses, outcome records |
| `proposals/` | optional proposal, clarity, panel and response forms |
| `method/` | how the room operates |
| `.codex/` | local Codex configuration and adapted base instructions |

## why the split is where it is

the three that are easiest to collapse into each other are `goals/`,
`journal.md`, and `notes/` — and collapsing them is what makes a research
archive unusable.

a **goal** is a question as it stands now. it gets edited as the question
sharpens; its history is not interesting.

the **journal** is the opposite: chronological and append-only in spirit. it is
where a change of direction gets recorded *as* a change of direction, so that
six months later the reason a line was abandoned is still there.

a **note** is reusable evidence — an argument, an example, a computation, a
dead end worth not repeating. it is written to be read again out of context,
which is what separates it from a journal entry.

`canonical/` is narrower still: what is currently load-bearing, with its
support attached. a claim moves in when it is carrying weight and moves out
when it stops.

## method/

eight files, read when their subject applies rather than on every turn.

`culture.md` and `execution.md` cover how work proceeds. `reporting.md` and
`artifacts.md` cover what gets written down and in what form. `review.md` is
the longest — the certification process. `proposals.md` covers when a proposal
panel is called for, and when it is not. `resources.md` carries the adjustable
bounded-experiment policy, which is what lets a cheap uncertain computation
start without ceremony. `handoffs.md` is optional.
