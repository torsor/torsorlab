a folder you copy and then work in. it is a workspace for sustained
mathematical research with an agent, arranged so that understanding
accumulates in a form you can come back to — while you keep hold of the
questions, the interpretations, and where the effort goes.

the shape of the problem it addresses: a long research collaboration produces a
great deal of material, most of it provisional. some of it is load-bearing.
some of it was believed for a while and then wasn't. without somewhere for each
kind to live, it all collapses into one undifferentiated transcript, and the
next session has to re-derive what the last one knew.

## how it divides the work

seven places, each with one job.

`conventions.md` holds definitions, notation, and standing assumptions — read
first, every time. `goals/` holds the current questions, stated concisely and
edited as they sharpen. `journal.md` is chronological: the reasoning, and the
changes of direction. `notes/` holds arguments, examples, computations, and
informative dead ends. `canonical/` holds the claims currently load-bearing,
with their support. `review/` holds exact review units, reports, responses, and
outcomes. `proposals/` holds the optional proposal and panel forms.

`method/` sits beside these and describes how the room is meant to operate —
culture, execution, reporting, artifacts, review, proposals, adjustable
resources, and an optional handoff policy. a method file is read when its
subject applies, not on every turn.

## what it is protecting

**your ability to steer.** a direct instruction to start or proceed authorizes
that work; the agent does not answer it with a proposal panel instead. small
examples, source checks and toy computations do not need a proposal at all. and
an exploratory exchange is not silently converted into a mandate to finish a
theorem.

**the difference between kinds of evidence.** a proposed argument, a finite
computation, an imported theorem, cleaned exposition, and an independently
reviewed argument are distinguished, and a dependent conclusion is never
recorded as more secure than the input it rests on.

**independent certification.** blind referees see an exact, self-contained
review unit — no author confidence signals, no prior votes. review proceeds by
natural claims and applications, so an accepted conditional calculation is not
mistaken for verification of its hypotheses.

**the ordinary register.** confidence language appears where it informs a
mathematical claim. it does not get appended to every conversation as a
compulsory ledger.
