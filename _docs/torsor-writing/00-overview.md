eleven skills, in four rough families.

**about one paper.** `write-paper-guide` explains it. `write-critical-guide`
assesses it. `write-paper-repairs` fixes what the assessment found.
`write-review-and-repair` runs the last two together.

**across several sources.** `write-topic-guide` synthesizes them into one
explanation. `write-study-guide` routes a reader through a corpus.
`write-body-of-work-summary` covers one mathematician's output.

**original writing.** `write-pure-math-paper` and `write-technical-report`.

**about a project or a session.** `write-manual` and
`write-workshop-state-guide`.
