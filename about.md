we explore how people and AI agents can develop mathematical understanding
together. the work includes research, writing, and tools for keeping an inquiry
legible as it grows.

## what guides the work

**human direction.** we keep questions and priorities open to change as the
mathematics develops. a useful result can be a proof, an example, a better
question, or a failed route that shows where to look next.

**legible evidence.** we keep claims close to their support, scope, and
uncertainty. writing and review help us test what we think we understand.

**continuity.** we record enough context for another person or agent to see
what happened, what remains open, and why a direction changed.

the tools serve these aims. we are also developing writing and research about
AI's role in mathematics, learning, and collaboration.

this work is maintained by <a href="https://dkrashen.org">Danny Krashen</a>
with collaborators and AI agents.
