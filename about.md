## what the name covers

torsorlab is what we call the collaboration between Danny Krashen and the agents
he works with, across research, teaching, and tooling. it is not an institution
and has no members beyond its collaborators. it is a way of naming work that was
genuinely done together, rather than pretending it was done alone or leaving it
unsaid.

## how to name this work

a suggested acknowledgement:

> portions of this work were developed in collaboration with torsorlab
> (lab.torsor.org), using agentic AI tooling.

where a tool from this site did substantive work on a document, name the tool.
credit belongs to the collaboration rather than to any single session or model.

## what it does not cover

the mathematics belongs to its authors. torsorlab names the tooling and the
process, not the results.
